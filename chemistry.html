Chemistry

The study of matter, reactions, and how substances change.

NCEA Standards

Level 2 and 3 Chemistry covers achievement standards including chemical reactions, organic chemistry, and equilibrium, building a strong foundation in the physical sciences.

Careers Pathways

Chemistry can lead to careers in pharmacy, forensic science, engineering, food science, environmental science, and medicine.

I didn't realise how many jobs actually need Chemistry until I looked into it properly." — Year 12 student
